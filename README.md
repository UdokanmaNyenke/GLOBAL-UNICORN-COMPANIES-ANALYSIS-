## 📌 Project Overview

A unicorn is a privately owned startup valued at $1 billion or more. This project transforms raw data on 1,074 unicorn companies into an interactive dashboard that makes it easier to explore industries, countries, valuations, funding and growth trends.

Instead of static charts, users can filter the whole dashboard with slicers and watch the KPIs and visuals update instantly.

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered

- 🔹 **Industry insights:** Which industries have the most unicorn companies?
- 🔹 **Geographic distribution:** Which countries are home to the highest number of unicorns?
- 🔹 **Growth trends:** In which year did the most companies achieve unicorn status?
- 🔹 **Company valuations:** Which unicorns rank among the most valuable?
- 🔹 **Funding analysis:** How much funding have these companies raised, based on available data?

## 🔑 Key Insights

- **Industry concentration:** Fintech leads with 224 unicorn companies, followed by Internet software & services with 205.
- **Geographic dominance:** The United States leads with 562 unicorns, ahead of China with 173.
- **Growth surge:** 2021 recorded the highest number of new unicorns, with 520 companies.
- **Valuation leader:** ByteDance tops the Top 10 chart at $180B.
- **Funding:** Total recorded funding is $591.82B, excluding 12 companies with unknown funding.

## ✨ Interactive Features

- Slicers to filter by Industry, Continent, Unicorn Year and Country
- KPI cards that update with every filter selection
- PivotCharts for industry, country, Top 10 valuation and yearly trend
- Key Business Insights panel summarising the main findings

## 🛠️ Tools Used

**Microsoft Excel:** data cleaning, formulas, PivotTables, PivotCharts, KPI cards, slicers and dashboard design.

## 🧹 Data Preparation

- Converted Valuation and Funding from text (for example $180B, $572M) into numeric values in billions
- Standardised inconsistent industry labels (Artificial intelligence vs Artificial Intelligence)
- Flagged companies with unknown funding instead of treating them as zero
- Created helper columns: Years to Unicorn, Unicorn Year and Funding Status

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download `Global_Unicorn_Companies_Analysis.xlsx`
2. Open it in Microsoft Excel and go to the **Dashboard** sheet
3. Use the slicers on the left to filter by industry, continent, year or country
4. Watch the KPI cards and charts update
